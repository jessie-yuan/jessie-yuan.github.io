Jessie Yuan

I am a senior studying computer science at Carnegie Mellon, broadly interested in human-robot interaction and robot learning.

I am fortunate to be working with Andrea Bajcsy on uncertainty quantification and continual learning in VLM-based robotic policy steering. Previously, I had the pleasure of working with Zackory Erickson on developing novel interfaces for physically assistive robots.
